# ML Breadth

A common interview round in ML interview is ML breadth, where the interview is trying to gauge your understanding across a wide range of ML topics. Usually this is done by quizzing you to explain concepts or answer when to use certain techniques or perform calculations. This section contains a collection of questions and answers that are asked in ML breadth interviews.
